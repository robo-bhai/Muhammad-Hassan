Hum kya maloomat lete hain

  • Naam, phone number, email

Istemal

Ye maloomat sirf order poora karne, delivery, tracking aur madad dene ke liye istemal hoti hain.

Share karna

Aap ka data bechte nahi. Seller ko order poora karne ke liye aap ka naam, phone aur (rider assign hone tak) address nazar aata hai. Rider ko address aur phone sirf us delivery ke dauran nazar aate hain jab tak wo chal rahi ho. Order mukammal hone ke baad seller ko address nahi dikhta aur phone number masked ho jata hai.

Hifazat

Pori site HTTPS par chalti hai. Aap ke address aur dusri hasaas maloomat database mein encrypted form mein rakhi jati hain. Phir bhi koi bhi system 100% mehfooz hone ki zamanat nahi de sakta.
